About the publication

Clearer questions for people who care about animals.

Young Leonidas is an independent educational publication about peptide biology, veterinary evidence, and the limits of translating research across species.

Why Young Leonidas exists

The publication is named for Leonidas, a senior husky whose continued curiosity inspired a deeper interest in how animals age, recover, and maintain quality of life. His story is the reason for the questions—not evidence that a treatment works.

Online discussions about peptides often move too quickly from a molecular mechanism to a promise. Young Leonidas takes the slower route: identify the species, study design, endpoint, regulatory status, and unanswered questions before drawing a conclusion.

What the editorial byline means

“Young Leonidas Editorial” identifies the publication as the author. It does not represent a claim that an article was written or reviewed by a veterinarian. When a qualified specialist contributes to or reviews an article, that person’s name, relevant credentials, role, and review date will be displayed on the article with permission.

What readers can expect

  • Clear separation between established veterinary use, clinical research, preclinical evidence, mechanism discussions, and unsupported claims.
  • Links to primary research, regulatory agencies, veterinary manuals, universities, and professional organizations where appropriate.
  • No individualized diagnosis, dosing, sourcing, or treatment instructions.
  • Visible publication and update dates when material changes are made.
