Privacy Policy for youngleonidas.com

Last updated: April 19, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and disclosed when you visit youngleonidas.com. This website is a simple landing page for the Young Leonidas presence on X and is hosted using GitHub Pages.

Information Collected

This site does not ask visitors to create accounts, submit payment information, or directly provide personal information through web forms.

Like most websites, limited technical information may be processed automatically by hosting and infrastructure providers, such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and basic request logs needed to operate and secure the site.

How Information May Be Used

Any automatically processed technical information may be used to:

  • serve and secure the website,
  • monitor site availability and performance, and
  • diagnose abuse, errors, or technical issues.

Cookies and Tracking

This site is intended to be a lightweight static website and does not intentionally deploy custom analytics, advertising cookies, or behavioral tracking scripts at this time.

Third-party platforms or infrastructure providers may use their own operational cookies or similar technologies as part of delivering content or handling security and performance.

Third-Party Links

This website links to third-party services, including X. If you click those links, your interaction with those services is governed by their own terms, privacy policies, and practices, not this site.

Data Sharing

This site does not sell visitor information. Information may be processed by service providers involved in hosting, content delivery, security, or domain infrastructure to the extent needed to operate the website.

Data Retention

Because this site does not maintain user accounts or a customer database, any retained information would generally be limited to infrastructure, access, or security logs retained by service providers according to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, use the public contact channel associated with the Young Leonidas X profile.
