Privacy policy

A lightweight site with minimal data collection.

Last updated July 21, 2026

Overview

This policy explains information handling when you visit youngleonidas.com. The current site is a static educational publication hosted through GitHub Pages.

Information you provide

The current site has no user accounts, checkout, comments, contact forms, or newsletter form. It does not ask you to provide payment information, pet medical records, or other personal information.

Technical information

Hosting, domain, and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs to deliver and protect the site.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not intentionally install custom analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking cookies. This policy will be updated before analytics or marketing technologies are introduced.

External links

Articles link to external research, regulatory, university, and veterinary websites. Those services have their own privacy practices and may collect information when you visit them.

Sharing and sale

Young Leonidas does not sell visitor personal information. Technical data may be processed by providers only as needed to host, secure, and deliver the site.

Children

The site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Future commerce

If accounts, email subscriptions, analytics, or a store are added, this policy will be revised before those features launch to describe the information collected, purpose, providers, retention, and available choices.

Security and retention

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Because the current site does not maintain a customer database, retained information is generally limited to provider infrastructure and security logs under provider policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The revised date will appear at the top of the page.
