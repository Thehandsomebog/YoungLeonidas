## Wikipedia dead-link research

Wikipedia's public search currently returns hundreds of peptide pages with
dead-link templates. Three clusters overlap with Young Leonidas topics:

| Wikipedia article | Dead-link context | Relevant Young Leonidas page | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Diabetes in dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diabetes_in_dogs) | Dead Zenodo convenience URLs attached to peer-reviewed studies on gestational diabetes and genetic susceptibility | `articles/insulin-dogs-cats.html` | Not a valid replacement. Preserve the journal citations and DOI links; remove or repair only the dead convenience URLs. |
| [Diabetes in cats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diabetes_in_cats) | FDA pages about Bexacat and two feline diabetes drugs are tagged dead | `articles/insulin-dogs-cats.html` | The FDA URLs are live again, so the correct fix is to remove the stale dead-link templates after verification—not replace FDA with Young Leonidas. |
| [Beta thymosins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_thymosins) | Old Pfam URLs supporting WH2-family database claims | `articles/tissue-repair-research.html` | Not a valid replacement. The claim needs a current Pfam/InterPro database record or peer-reviewed source. |

Young Leonidas should not be inserted as a replacement citation merely because
the topic is related. The site is self-published and currently does not provide
the same primary evidence as the sources above. Wikipedia explicitly advises
site owners not to add their own links for SEO and discourages direct editing
where there is a conflict of interest:

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:External_links#Advertising_and_conflicts_of_interest
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources#Self-published_sources_(online_and_paper)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Link_rot

If Young Leonidas later publishes independently reviewed work by named,
credentialed veterinary experts that directly supports a claim, the appropriate
route is a disclosed suggestion on the article's talk page for independent
editors to assess.

prioritize:

1. Queries with impressions, average position 5–20, and low CTR.
